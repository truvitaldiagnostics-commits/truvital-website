Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers before you book.

Is fasting needed before the test?

It depends on the test. Fasting Blood Sugar, lipid profile and some other tests need an overnight fast (usually 8 to 10 hours, water is fine). Tests like CBC, HbA1c, vitamin D and thyroid usually do not need fasting. Our team will tell you exactly what to do when you book on WhatsApp.

When do reports arrive?

Reports are sent as a digital PDF on WhatsApp, usually the same day once the sample is processed. Some tests may take longer.

What payment methods do you accept?

Payment details are confirmed with our team on WhatsApp when you book.

Which areas get free home collection?

Free home sample collection is available in Miyapur, Allwyn Colony, Hafeezpet, Madeenaguda, Matrusri Nagar, Nizampet and JP Nagar.

How does home collection work?

Book on WhatsApp or call us with your name, area and preferred time. Our certified technician B. Tarun Kumar visits your home, collects the sample using sterilized single-use equipment, and stores it in cold-chain boxes for transport to the lab. Your PDF report comes to WhatsApp.
